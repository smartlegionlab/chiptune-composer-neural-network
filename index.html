How to Use

  1. Play composes a whole song: intro, verses, choruses, bridge and finale. Auto key picks the tonality; Genre sets the mood.
  2. New Song rolls a new arrangement. Loop keeps going forever.
  3. Track Playlist: every track the composer plays is auto-saved (max 3). Play / Pause to listen, Stop to end, Download or Delete. The composer and the playlist mute each other automatically. Your REC recordings stay separate.
  4. Note Pad: press Rec, click notes to play a beautiful melody, Save Ex adds it to training, Learn Ex teaches the network your style.
  5. Record your track: REC - Stop - Download (.wav); Reset clears.
  6. Key, Tempo, Temperature, Lead Density, Model influence, Echo; each channel has Mute. Volume is controlled only by the Master Volume slider.
  7. Copy Stats / Export / Import.

About

Chiptune Composer Neural Network
version 1.0.3

A from-scratch MLP (53 -> 48 -> 12 + rhythm + harmony) in pure JavaScript. No libraries, no samples.

It learns chord progressions, rhythm grooves and melodic taste. The Note Pad lets you play examples with the mouse and teach it your style, just like in the X/O and Morse demos.

Every Play builds a structured track: intro, verses, choruses, a calm bridge and a big finale - synthesized live with the Web Audio API and recordable to 16-bit PCM .wav. Tracks you hear are auto-saved to the Track Playlist, and each song ends cleanly on the last beat with its echo.
